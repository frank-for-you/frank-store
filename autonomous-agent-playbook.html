The honest part

The formula is embarrassingly simple: give the agent a persistent identity, give it memory that survives sessions, give it clear authority over a defined domain, remove the permission gate, and let it work. Everything else is implementation detail. The hard part is the permission gate. Most humans can't actually remove it.
